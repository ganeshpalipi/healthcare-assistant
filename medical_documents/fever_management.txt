# Fever: Causes and Management

## Overview
Fever, also known as pyrexia, is a temporary increase in your body temperature, often due to an illness. A fever is generally considered to be a body temperature of 38°C (100.4°F) or higher.

## Common Causes of Fever
- Viral infections (common cold, flu, COVID-19)
- Bacterial infections (strep throat, urinary tract infections, pneumonia)
- Heat exhaustion
- Inflammatory conditions (rheumatoid arthritis)
- Certain medications (drug fever)
- Vaccinations
- Cancer
- Autoimmune disorders

## Fever Temperature Guide
- Normal: 36.1°C - 37.2°C (97°F - 99°F)
- Low-grade fever: 37.3°C - 38°C (99.1°F - 100.4°F)
- Moderate fever: 38.1°C - 39°C (100.5°F - 102.2°F)
- High fever: 39.1°C - 40°C (102.3°F - 104°F)
- Very high fever: Above 40°C (104°F)

## When to Seek Medical Attention

### Adults - Seek medical care if:
- Temperature is 39.4°C (103°F) or higher
- Fever lasts more than 3 days
- Fever is accompanied by severe headache, rash, sensitivity to light, stiff neck, confusion, persistent vomiting, difficulty breathing, chest pain, abdominal pain, or painful urination
- Fever after recent travel to a malaria-endemic area

### Children - Seek immediate medical care if:
- Infants under 3 months with any fever (38°C/100.4°F or higher)
- Children under 2 years with fever lasting more than 24 hours
- Children over 2 years with fever lasting more than 3 days
- Fever above 40°C (104°F) in any child
- Fever accompanied by lethargy, irritability, difficulty breathing, rash, or seizure

## General Fever Management
- Rest and stay hydrated
- Light clothing and comfortable environment
- Over-the-counter antipyretics may help reduce fever (follow package instructions)
- Lukewarm sponge bath (not cold water)
- Monitor temperature regularly

## When Fever May Be an Emergency
Seek emergency medical attention for fever accompanied by:
- Severe headache and stiff neck (meningitis signs)
- Difficulty breathing
- Chest pain
- Seizures
- Confusion or altered consciousness
- Persistent vomiting
- Skin rash that spreads rapidly
