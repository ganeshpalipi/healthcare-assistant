# Common Cold and Influenza (Flu)

## Overview
The common cold and influenza (flu) are both respiratory illnesses caused by different viruses. While they share some symptoms, they are caused by different viruses and have different levels of severity.

## Common Cold
The common cold is a viral infection of your upper respiratory tract (nose and throat). It is usually harmless, although it might not feel that way. Many types of viruses can cause a common cold.

### Symptoms of Common Cold:
- Runny or stuffy nose
- Sore throat
- Cough
- Congestion
- Slight body aches or mild headache
- Sneezing
- Low-grade fever
- Generally feeling unwell (malaise)

### Duration
Cold symptoms usually appear 1 to 3 days after exposure to a cold-causing virus and typically last for about 7 to 10 days.

### Common Causes
- Rhinoviruses (most common)
- Coronaviruses (not COVID-19)
- Adenoviruses
- Enteroviruses

### General Management
- Rest
- Stay hydrated (drink water, juice, clear broths)
- Over-the-counter cold medications may help relieve symptoms
- Use a humidifier
- Gargle with salt water for sore throat

## Influenza (Flu)
Influenza is a viral infection that attacks your respiratory system — your nose, throat, and lungs. Influenza is commonly called the flu, but it's not the same as stomach "flu" viruses that cause diarrhea and vomiting.

### Symptoms of Influenza:
- Fever or feeling feverish/chills (often high, 38°C or 100.4°F or higher)
- Cough
- Sore throat
- Runny or stuffy nose
- Muscle or body aches
- Headaches
- Fatigue (tiredness)
- Some people may have vomiting and diarrhea (more common in children)

### Duration
Most people who get the flu recover within 1 to 2 weeks. However, serious complications can develop.

### Complications of Influenza:
- Pneumonia
- Bronchitis
- Asthma flare-ups
- Ear infections
- Heart problems

### Prevention
- Annual flu vaccination
- Frequent hand washing
- Avoiding close contact with sick people
- Covering coughs and sneezes

## When to See a Doctor
Seek medical attention if you experience:
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
- Persistent chest pain or pressure
- New confusion
- Severe or persistent vomiting
- Flu-like symptoms that improve but then return with fever and worse cough
